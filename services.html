01 / Strategy & Governance

Set the direction. Govern the investment.

Digital transformation requires more than a collection of initiatives. It needs a clear strategy, informed investment decisions, effective oversight and a shared understanding of what success looks like.

We help Boards and executive teams establish the strategic and governance foundations needed to lead transformation confidently.

Our offerings

Digital Transformation Strategy & Roadmap

Develop a clear transformation vision, assess digital maturity, prioritise initiatives and define a practical roadmap supported by an investment case.

Technology, Security & AI Governance

Strengthen technology oversight, cybersecurity governance, AI accountability and risk management in alignment with organisational objectives and relevant standards.

Enterprise Architecture

Define or independently review the business, information and technology structures required to support strategic priorities.

Benefits Realisation

Establish clear measures of success, connect transformation investments to expected business outcomes and support the tracking of realised value.

Employee & Digital Skills Audit

Change Leadership

Workforce Transition Management

Common questions

Questions we hear first.

What does Flex Digital do?

Flex Digital is a Ghana-based digital transformation advisory firm. We help Boards, executive teams and institutions shape, govern and deliver transformation through three connected service lines: Strategy & Governance, People & Leadership, and Delivery & Solutions.

Who does Flex Digital work with?

We work with Boards and Board committees, Chief Executive Officers, Chief Information and Technology Officers, risk and compliance leaders, HR and People leaders, and transformation and programme teams in businesses and institutions across Africa.

Is Flex Digital independent of technology vendors?

Yes. Our advice is vendor-neutral and grounded in each client's strategic priorities and risk appetite. We recommend approaches and technologies based on their suitability and potential value, not a commitment to particular vendors.

Which frameworks and standards does Flex Digital reference?

Where relevant, our technology, security and AI governance work can reference established frameworks such as COBIT, NIST CSF and ISO 27001.

How does an engagement with Flex Digital work?

Our methodology follows four stages: Assess, Engage, Design and Mobilise. The scope, duration and outputs of each engagement are tailored to the organisation's requirements.

Where is Flex Digital based and which markets do you serve?

Flex Digital is based in Ghana and serves organisations across Africa. Our consultants have worked across eight markets in Africa and Europe.

Use the contact page, email ebo@flexdigital.africa or call +233 277 55 14 14. We begin with an initial conversation, followed where appropriate by a needs assessment and a tailored proposal.

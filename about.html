About Flex Digital

Experience that transforms.

We are a Ghana-based digital transformation advisory firm helping businesses and institutions make better decisions, navigate change and deliver lasting value.

Who we are

Practitioners in the Boardroom. Partners in execution.

Flex Digital was founded by senior practitioners with extensive experience leading and governing business transformation across Africa and Europe.

Our backgrounds span banking, telecommunications, fintech, technology leadership, organisational change and digital product delivery.

We understand the strategic responsibilities of Boards and executive teams, as well as the operational realities of implementing transformation across complex organisations.

This combination of perspectives enables us to connect ambition with execution, ensuring that strategic decisions translate into meaningful, measurable outcomes.

What drives us

Helping African institutions thrive in the digital era.

Our ambition is to become the digital transformation partner of choice for businesses and institutions across Africa.

We believe that organisations should be able to navigate technological change with confidence, make informed investment decisions and build the internal capabilities needed to succeed.

Our role is to provide independent advice, experienced leadership and practical solutions that help make that possible.

Our people

Led by experience. United by purpose.

Our leadership brings together complementary strengths in strategic transformation, people and culture, and programme delivery.

ER

Ebo Richardson

Co-founder & Principal Consultant

Award-winning transformation leader with almost three decades of C-suite, Board and advisory experience across banking, telecommunications and fintech in Africa and Europe.

His experience includes senior technology and transformation leadership at Barclays, Millicom and Equity Group, and the leadership of Barclays Ghana's transition to Absa.

He also brings governance experience from serving on regulated Boards and chairing a Technology and Cybersecurity Committee.

At Flex Digital, Ebo leads strategic advisory, transformation governance and executive engagement.

AR

Akua Richardson

Co-founder & Managing Consultant

Chartered CIPD People and Change Leader with more than two decades of human resources leadership experience in complex UK organisations.

Her professional experience spans organisational design, people strategy, leadership development and change management, with roles involving organisations such as British Telecom, TalkTalk, Gamma and Manchester City Council.

At Flex Digital, Akua leads People and Culture, helping organisations prepare their people, structures and leadership for sustainable transformation.

CL

Collins Lartey

Co-founder & Delivery Lead

Project management, digital transformation and product development professional with over a decade of experience delivering technology-driven initiatives across education, financial services and digital technology.

His experience includes product and project leadership at Finlite Group, Eneza Education and TDI Global, and the development of platforms including FinMock, which reached more than one million learners, and the My3D Career Assessment Platform across Ghana and Nigeria.

He is also the founder of Bold Vision MultiTech, where he leads SME digital transformation and technology innovation.

At Flex Digital, Collins leads delivery, translating transformation plans into coordinated programmes and measurable results.
